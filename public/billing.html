Billing & Invoices

Membership payments and billing records are managed securely by Stripe. Knightfall never stores your card number.

Checking membership…

Premium membership is optional. The community and free Deck Atelier skins remain available without subscribing.

Invoice history

When billing is active, use Manage billing & invoices for Stripe-hosted receipts, invoices, payment-method updates, and subscription cancellation. For individual business invoices, use the Stripe Invoicing Dashboard if you're a Knightfall billing administrator.

Creating a subscription or an invoice never automatically changes your community permissions. Membership access is granted only after Stripe confirms the subscription state through a verified webhook.
