# Third-party source attribution

The casino mini-game engine in this directory is adapted from **Casino by AI** by Lemelson:

- Source: https://github.com/Lemelson/casino-by-ai
- Upstream license: MIT (see [LICENSE](./LICENSE))
- Upstream features: slots, blackjack, European roulette, and Plinko
- Local changes: Ash-Fall / Chaos Cartridge integration, site navigation, responsive canvas sizing, removal of third-party analytics, and play-money disclosure.

All chips are virtual, have no cash value, and cannot be purchased, sold, deposited, or withdrawn. This is an arcade simulation, not a real-money gambling service.
