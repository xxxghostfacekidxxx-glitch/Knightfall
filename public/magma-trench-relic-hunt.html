Heat is the real enemy

  • Harpoon fires quickly with low heat. Drill hits harder and penetrates armor. Magma Torpedo is powerful but generates serious heat.
  • At 100% heat the cannon locks briefly. Vent Burst has a cooldown, so don't waste it when the barrel is already cool.
  • The Furnace King has layered armor. A torpedo deals heavy damage, while the exposed glowing core takes extra damage after its armor breaks.
  • Depth increases as you survive. Deeper creatures pay more, but have more health and move unpredictably.
