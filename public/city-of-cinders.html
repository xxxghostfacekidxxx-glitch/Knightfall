How to ruin a perfectly good skyline

  • Move: A/D or arrow keys. Use the on-screen buttons on mobile.
  • Smash: Space or hold PUNCH. Face a façade and attack its individual floors: windows crack, walls open, structural damage spreads, and the whole tower can collapse.
  • Climb: Hold W or ↑ against a building wall (hold CLIMB on mobile). Reach the top to pull yourself onto the roof. Hold S or ↓ to descend.
  • Jump: J, X or JUMP. Leap off walls, rooftops, or evade gunfire.
  • Rage roar: E or ROAR when the rage meter reaches 100%. It wrecks nearby structures and clears projectiles.
  • Win: Wreck all 12 buildings. Survive long enough to make the skyline a historical rumor.
