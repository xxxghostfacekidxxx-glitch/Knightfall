How to ruin a perfectly good skyline

  • Move: A/D or arrow keys. Use the on-screen buttons on mobile.
  • Punch: Space or the PUNCH button. Get close to a building or military unit.
  • Jump: W, ↑, or JUMP. Helps dodge incoming fire.
  • Rage roar: E or ROAR when the rage meter reaches 100%. It wrecks nearby structures and clears projectiles.
  • Win: Wreck all 12 buildings. Survive long enough to make the skyline a historical rumor.
