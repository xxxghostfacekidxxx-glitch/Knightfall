Egyptian language laboratory

Egyptian hieroglyphic writing combines signs for sounds, words and meaning categories. The 24 traditional one-consonant signs are a beginner inventory, not the entire system. Ancient vowels and exact historical sounds are often uncertain. Transliteration represents written signs; translation conveys meaning.

Writing systems and historical stages

Hieroglyphic

Formal and monumental signs as well as written textual traditions; learn uniliterals, biliterals, triliterals, word signs and classifiers.

Hieratic

Cursive Egyptian writing for many administrative, literary and religious manuscripts. Not simply an alternate modern alphabet.

Demotic

Later cursive script and associated language stage; compare documentary and literary evidence.

Coptic

Late stage of Egyptian written largely with Greek-derived letters plus additional signs. Sahidic and Bohairic are among its literary varieties. Pronunciation and liturgical traditions differ.

Theban

Renaissance occult substitution cipher, not pharaonic Egyptian. Open the existing Theban tool.
