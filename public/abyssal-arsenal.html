How to play

  • Move your mouse or finger to aim. Hold down to fire rapidly.
  • Choose cannon power. Stronger shots cost more but deal more damage.
  • Fish have hidden durability and distinct movement patterns. Watch their behavior and keep pressure on tougher targets; no health bars clutter the table.
  • Trigger Chain Blast to zap several nearby targets, if you can afford the charge.
  • Boss waves bring the biggest targets. Manage credits and don't waste heavy shots on tiny fry.
