HOW TO SURVIVE

A number counts mines in the eight neighboring tiles. Right-click to flag, or turn on Flag mode for touch. Click an open number with enough neighboring flags to clear the remaining neighbors; wrong flags can still detonate a mine.

One flare opens a safe tile per field and costs 200 points. *Charges left means mines minus flags, not confirmed danger. On small screens, scroll larger boards sideways.
