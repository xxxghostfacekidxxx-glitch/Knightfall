HOW TO PLAY

Watch the numbered rune flashes, then repeat what you saw by tapping or pressing 1–4. Cursed mode reverses the entire pattern every fourth round. Classic preserves the straight sequence from Ember Echo and Void Sequence. A mistake ends the run. Pausing replays the current pattern when resumed.

Local play available.
