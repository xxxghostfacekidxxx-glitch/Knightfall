Ash Tiles

Slide the fifteen tiles into order. A solvable scramble, animated stones, and one very inconvenient empty space.

CONTROL DECK

MOVES 0BEST SCORE 0
HOW TO PLAY

Click a tile next to the gap to slide it. Arrow keys move the empty space. New puzzles are scrambled through legal moves without immediately undoing each shuffle step. Show goal previews the completed board without changing your puzzle or awarding points.

Local play available.
