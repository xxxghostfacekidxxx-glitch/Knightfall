    HOW TO PLAY

    Each player chooses a work or college path and career. Players alternate spins of 1–10 spaces. Collect your salary whenever you pass or land on Payday. Choose how to handle family, homes, careers, investments, and unexpected events. Debts reduce your retirement wealth. The richest overall net worth when everyone retires wins. Up to four local players, no account needed.

    An independent original game inspired by classic life-journey board-game concepts. Not affiliated with Hasbro or The Game of Life.
