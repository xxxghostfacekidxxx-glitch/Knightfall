HOW TO PLAY

Slide matching tiles together with arrows, WASD, swipe or the direction pad. Three consecutive merging moves ignite a Cinder Cascade bonus. A non-merging move breaks the chain. Reach 2048 and keep going. Your run saves locally; signed-in cloud saves can also be loaded.

Local play available.
