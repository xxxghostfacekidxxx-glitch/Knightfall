# Cinderwild artwork and Ashen Village

The village layout and art are adapted from [Ninja Adventure](https://github.com/pixel-boy/NinjaAdventure/tree/6ac78232d5aedcc85ce5f27d060ea92366f7c24a) by Pixel-Boy and AAA, from the [CC0 1.0 Ninja Adventure asset pack](https://pixel-boy.itch.io/ninja-adventure-asset-pack).

- `village.png` composites the authored floor, detail, and wall tiles in columns −11 through 12 and rows −10 through 7 of `content/map/map_village.tscn`. Cinderwild uses the matching wall footprint, its own encounter tiles, and a new northern entrance. Godot scripts, original mobs, and scene-based props are omitted.
- `terrain.png` and `props.png` are the upstream floor and abandoned village atlases.
- `trainer.png` and `warden.png` crop the first four animation rows from the upstream blue ninja and samurai sheets.

[CC0 1.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

`twilight-clearing.webp` is an original generated illustration for Cinderwild's battle arena.
