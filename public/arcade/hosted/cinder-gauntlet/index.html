Choose a hero and enter the dungeon.
Move: WASD / arrows. Aim: mouse, touch, or last movement direction. Attack: Space, click/touch arena, or ATTACK. Collect keys, food and treasure. Destroy the glowing enemy generators. Reach the green exit to descend. Each floor becomes harder.
Original code and procedural artwork for Ash-Fall. No Gauntlet franchise assets used.
