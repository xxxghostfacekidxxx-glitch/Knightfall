How to play

  1. Tap Garden or Bakery for free ingredients.
  2. Drag matching items together, or tap one then another.
  3. When you own a dish a customer wants, tap Serve for tips and XP.
  4. Unlock the Sweetshop at level 3. Every fifth order starts a 45-second tip frenzy.

Swapping and rearranging is free. Recycle any selected item to clear space. Your kitchen restores energy while you're away.
