How to play

  1. Tap a Garden or Bakery generator on the grid twice to harvest, or drag it to customize your layout. Each harvest costs one energy.
  2. Drag matching items together, or tap one then another.
  3. When you own a dish a customer wants, tap Serve for tips and XP. Refresh one unfinished request for 35 earned coins if you get stuck.
  4. Unlock the Sweetshop at level 3. Every fifth order starts a 45-second tip frenzy.

Need a hand? Find a match highlights two ingredients for free. Earn Magic Whisks by serving every three orders for an automatic merge. The Secret Pantry starts with two free pockets and unlocks another at levels 4, 7, 10 and 13. Stored dishes can be served, and everything survives saves. Swapping and rearranging is free. Recycle any selected item to clear space. Complete workboard quests and discover new recipes for extra coins and energy. Your kitchen restores energy while you're away.
