Lights Out: Afterburn

Extinguish the reactor lanterns. Every tap flips a cross of neighboring lights.

CONTROL DECK

MOVES 0LIGHTS LEFT 0HINTS 0BEST SCORE 0
HOW TO PLAY

Each tap toggles its own lantern and the adjacent lanterns above, below, left and right. Every puzzle starts from a solved board and is scrambled using legal flips, so a solution always exists. Hints make one known solution move and cost 50 points. Undo restores your previous board.

Local play available.
