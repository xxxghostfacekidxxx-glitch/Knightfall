Pilot the ship, blast the rocks, and try not to turn your spaceship into a brief philosophical statement. Move with ←/→, thrust with ↑, fire with Space, and pause with P. On touchscreens, use the controls below.

Adapted for Chaos Cartridge from Canvas Asteroids by Doug McInnes. The upstream game logic is retained with local presentation, touch controls, and fullscreen support. Sound assets are intentionally omitted from this hosted build; gameplay does not depend on them.
