READY, SWAMP RANGER?

Mind the bog.

Tap or click critters to score. Fireflies are bonus targets. The grumpy frogs are decoys and cost points. Six shots per magazine, auto-reload after a short pause.

How to play: Tap / click targets. Marsh mice are worth 10, fireflies 25, and decoy frogs subtract 15. Every target hit uses one round. Misses do not consume ammo. Best score is saved on this device. This original build does not yet award account-wide scores or currency.
