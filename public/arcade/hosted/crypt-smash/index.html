HOW TO PLAY

Hit the glowing glitches. Twenty seconds, no friendly decoys.

Every five consecutive hits raises your multiplier, up to ×4. An escaped target, empty tap, or friendly hit breaks the combo. Accuracy counts successful hits divided by all taps. Mode records stay separate.

Keyboard: use 1–9 for the portals (top left to bottom right). In Exterminator mode use Tab and Enter to select bugs. Pause or leave the tab to freeze the round; resume when ready.
