# Embervale — A Fox Tale

Original author: **Nicholas Aryel Ferreira**. License: **MIT**, included verbatim in `LICENSE`.

- Source: https://github.com/NicholasAryelFerreira/embervale
- Original browser demo: https://nicholasaryelferreira.github.io/embervale/
- Knightfall additions: responsive scaling, custom neon browser frame, fullscreen + arcade return links, native Pointer Events D-pad, Sword/Talk, Item, Inventory/Pause buttons, and accessibility labels.
- All upstream game levels, game engine, sprites, effects, music synthesis and story are retained without modification except for responsive canvas sizing.
- Dependencies: none. Assets and soundtrack are generated in JavaScript. Self-hosted and offline-capable when downloaded with all files.
- Independent original adventure, inspired by top-down action adventures; not an official Nintendo or Zelda franchise product.

## Knightfall original expansion (2026-11-02)

- **Six new eastern overworld screens** beyond Lakeside, reached by a rebuilt eight-tile bridge on the eastern shoreline.
- **Cinder Keep:** six interconnected rooms, persistent small-key door, Ember Wisp and Stone Guard enemies, the Cinder Warden boss and the **Ember Blade** (double sword damage).
- **Prism Catacombs:** four interconnected rooms, second key-gated route, the Prism Beast boss and the **Storm Bow** (three bolts per arrow spent).
- Existing browser save format/room coordinates are preserved. New achievements and treasure are stored with existing flags. New bosses do not trigger or replace the original ending.
- Additional enemy visuals and combat behavior are custom code, not Nintendo artwork.
- Mobile D-pad and attack buttons remain supported. New areas require a new game or traveling east from a continued save.
