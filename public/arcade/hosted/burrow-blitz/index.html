DIG YOUR WAY OUT

Collect ten neon gems and reach the exit. Rocks fall when the dirt beneath them is cleared. Physics remains deeply unhelpful.

Keyboard: arrows or WASD. Touch D-pad included. Clear dirt to open paths; rocks can fall into excavated spaces and crush you. Original Knightfall digging game, not a port of Digger.
