HOW TO PLAY

Reveal two cards per move. Matching pairs stay visible; mismatches turn back over after a moment. Finish in fewer moves for a better score. One two-second peek is available per board and costs 250 points.

Quick preserves the original six-pair board. Classic combines Crypt Pairs and Madhouse Match. Challenge adds twelve pairs. Use Tab and Enter, or the arrow keys, to play by keyboard.

Local play available.
