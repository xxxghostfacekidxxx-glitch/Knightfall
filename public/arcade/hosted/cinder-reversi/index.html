HOW TO PLAY & KEYBOARD CONTROLS

Trap one or more enemy discs between two of yours, in any of eight directions. The trapped discs flip to your color. If you have no legal move, you pass; when neither player can move, the higher disc count wins.

U = undo your previous turn, H = show a possible move, R = restart, F = fullscreen, Escape = exit focus mode. Tapping a glowing legal square works on phones and tablets. Matches and local records save automatically in this browser.
