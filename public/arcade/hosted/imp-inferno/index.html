Take your seat

Every shot spends table credits. Shots open into nets, and a catch pays its multiplier × your shot cost. Hold to fire or switch on Auto.

Hold the table to fire. With Lock on, tap an imp to track it. Arrow keys aim, Space fires, 1–4 set power, Esc pauses. Table credits reset each session.

Higher-cost shots spread wider and have a better chance to catch. Credits are free and stay on this table.
