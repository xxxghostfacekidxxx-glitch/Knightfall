Take your seat

Every shot spends table credits. Shots open into nets, and a catch pays its multiplier × your shot cost. Hold to fire or switch on Auto.

Player 2 WASD aim · F fire · Q/E change power

Hold the table to fire. With Lock on, tap an imp to track it. Arrow keys aim, Space fires, 1–7 set power, Esc pauses. Table credits reset each session.

Catch guide

Common creatures: Coalbug 1× · Grinner 2× · Cinder Bat 3× · Ashwing 4× · Hellhound Pup 6× · Brute 8× · Skull Wisp 10× · Magma Grub 14×.

Rare beasts: Bone Serpent 24× · Gargoyle 45× · Infernal Hydra 60× · Furnace King 80×.

Fuse 15× explodes · Spark 20× chains · Prism 25× grants a Hellbeam · Drill 30× grants a piercing shot · Vortex 35× sweeps one nearby species.

Croupier 12× triggers the Furnace Wheel. It awards 5×–50× your catch shot cost in free table credits.

Firestorm 18× sends a wall of flame across the table that can catch up to 12 common imps.

Lit rails show where the next schools enter. Inferno Tide sweeps in from one side, Crossfire enters from both sides, and Chevron Rush converges near the center.

BOSS HUNTS: The Gargoyle (45×) dive-slams and calls Coalbugs; the three-headed Hydra (60×) unleashes Triple Hellfire and summons Skull Wisps; the towering Furnace King (80×) fires Crown Novas and summons Hellhounds.

Each boss has a shared armor meter. Even a resisted net shot cracks its armor, and the player whose shot breaks through gets the catch. Watch for the glowing WEAK POINT after a special attack: shots hit harder and catches become more likely for a few seconds.

Boss attacks spawn extra catchable creatures and visual shockwaves; they never deduct credits or disable shots. The King's entrance has a five-second warning and a demonic escort.

Shots bounce off the rails.

Higher-cost shots spread wider and have a better chance to catch. Credits are free and stay on this table.
