HOW TO PLAY

Tap to begin. While the panel says WAIT, hold your nerve. When it turns green and says GO, tap immediately. After each result, tap again for the next round.

Use touch, the left mouse button, Space, or Enter. Early taps retry the current round and cost points: 250 in Classic, 25 in After Dark. Completed rounds stay intact.

Switching tabs interrupts the current trial. Resume to receive a fresh signal; interrupted trials do not count. Each mode keeps its own record. Results vary with your device and input method.

Local play available.
