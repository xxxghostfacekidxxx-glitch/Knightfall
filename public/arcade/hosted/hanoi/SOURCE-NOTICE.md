# Tower of Hanoi source notice

This cabinet adapts the self-contained Tower of Hanoi puzzle from `hanoi/index.html` in [mashukui/web-games](https://github.com/mashukui/web-games).

- Upstream license: MIT. The complete license is retained in `LICENSE.txt`.
- Upstream source: https://github.com/mashukui/web-games
- Knightfall changes: Ash-Fall palette and navigation, removed the unrelated third-party downloader promotion and analytics loader, added account leaderboard submission on completed levels, account status feedback, and a direct issue-report link.
- The puzzle board is rendered with inline SVG/DOM elements. No external images, sound files, or remote game runtime dependencies were copied.
