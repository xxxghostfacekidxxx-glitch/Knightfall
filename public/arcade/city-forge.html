ASH-FALL CITY FORGE

Build your city, place vehicles and characters, then launch GTA-2W. Layouts auto-save in this browser. This is the 2D placement editor; 3D GLB imports use the game's Asset Editor.

Select a tile or object, then tap the map.

Placement tools

Use Road, Sidewalk, Park, Water, Building and Tower to paint ground. Place cars, police, trucks, trees, lights, barrels, pickups, pedestrians, and your player spawn on top. Eraser removes objects on a tile before removing ground.

Map units match GTA-2W: one tile is 4 world units. The pink and green buildings are original color placeholders, not imported 3D models.

Asset pathway

Our existing Devil Tools SVGs can become 2D decals or reference art. The game needs GLB 3D models to replace its built-in vehicles and characters. Do not treat an SVG as a working GLB.
