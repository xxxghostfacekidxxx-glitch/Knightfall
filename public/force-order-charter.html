Living Charters

Proposed original spiritual principles for community review.

Jedi Temple

Through knowledge, we ascend.

Principles

  1. Seek understanding before judgment.
  2. Practice compassion with consent.
  3. Exercise discipline without denying emotion.
  4. Serve without claiming ownership of others.
  5. Accept correction and repair harm.
  6. Teach without demanding agreement.

Voluntary dedication

I choose awareness over haste, understanding over certainty, and service without possession. I accept responsibility for my choices and remain willing to learn.

Order of the Sith

From ruin, we endure.

Principles

  1. Recognize passion without becoming its captive.
  2. Build strength through deliberate practice.
  3. Measure power by competence and freedom of choice.
  4. Pursue ambition without coercion.
  5. Accept consequences and revise failing strategies.
  6. Respect the sovereignty of others.

Voluntary dedication

I choose deliberate will over compulsion. I will confront my limitations, build my strength and own the consequences of my actions. My freedom does not require another person's submission.

Shared rights

Participation, dedication and withdrawal are voluntary. No payment, disclosure, obedience to AI, or surrender of personal autonomy is required. These drafts do not confer legal status, office or Academy rank.
