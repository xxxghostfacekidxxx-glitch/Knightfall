The archivist's method

Distinguish manuscript date, printed edition, attributed author, translator and later adaptation. Preserve source links and variants; do not present a modern rendering as an authentic manuscript. Add original commentary rather than copying protected editions. Use Egyptian Civilization for ancient Egyptian cultural history and Celestial Sanctum for celestial tools.
