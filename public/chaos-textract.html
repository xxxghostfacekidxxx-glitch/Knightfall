Feed the little demon a document. She'll turn its readable words into text you can inspect, copy, save, or explicitly hand to Miss Chaos for analysis.

Free and local first: your original files stay in this browser. PDF, Word, spreadsheet, slide, and image processing uses open-source libraries loaded on demand. The first OCR run downloads language data and needs an internet connection. No Amazon Textract account or paid API is used.
