TEXTRACT

Feed the little demon a document. She'll turn its readable words into text you can inspect, copy, save, or explicitly hand to Miss Chaos for analysis.

Private and free: files are processed in your browser. Text-layer PDFs and Office documents are supported. Pictures use on-device text detection where available, otherwise experimental OCR. Always verify important numbers.

Which documents can Textract read?

Selectable-text PDFs, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX and plain text work locally. Some scanned PDFs containing drawn JPEG or bounded Flate-compressed grayscale/RGB images can be read locally; other scanned-image encodings still require exporting pages as images. No cloud upload, paid OCR API, Tesseract, PDF.js or third-party runtime is used.

Image OCR is experimental and intended for simple, high-contrast printed Latin text. Google Photos cloud-only images may need saving to Files.

𓂀 Original Glyph Forge OCR works on image files and supported JPEG- or Flate-backed PDF pages. It does not render arbitrary PDF images, recognize handwriting reliably, or support every font. Verify extracted names, numbers, and dates.
