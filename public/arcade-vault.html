Snake in the Ashes

Minefield of Bad Ideas

Clear the board without detonating ten hidden mines. Numbers show nearby danger. You get one emergency flare per field: it opens a random safe tile, but the rest is on your detective work. On phones, Flag mode marks suspicious squares.
