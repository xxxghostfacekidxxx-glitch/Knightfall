A way to begin

In Jungian psychology, the shadow includes traits and feelings we have trouble recognizing in ourselves. That can include strengths we learned to hide.

  1. Describe. Write down what actually happened and what you noticed in yourself.
  2. Get curious. Ask what you needed, feared, or assumed. Leave room for more than one explanation.
  3. Choose one step. Name something small you could try, such as asking for time to think or practicing a boundary.

Use this as a self-reflection practice. You can skip a prompt or stop at any point.
