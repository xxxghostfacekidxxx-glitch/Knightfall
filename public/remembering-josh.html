Remembering Josh

Some of the people who knew Josh Van Patten started collecting rocks because of him. His excitement was contagious. A streak of color through a stone was reason enough to pick it up, turn it over, and show somebody else. After a while, the people around him were looking a little closer, too. Something they’d walked past a hundred times could stop them on the hundred-and-first.

Josh was a devoted father and the kind of friend people knew they could call. When someone needed him, he showed up. He brought his happiness with him, and he had a way of making room for somebody else in whatever had caught his interest that day. The rocks and crystals were one part of that generosity. The time he gave people was another.

Josh’s friendship helped inspire the completion of Knightfall. Here in the Sanctuary, a sea turtle carries his nickname, Crush, in his honor. Those who knew Josh have other ways of remembering him, too. Sometimes it’s as simple as finding a stone, turning it over, and thinking how much he would have loved it.
