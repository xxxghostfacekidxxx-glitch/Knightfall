Remembering Josh

I look at the ground a little differently because I knew Josh. He went rock hunting all the time and knew rocks and crystals inside out. What I loved was how much joy he got out of sharing what he knew. So many people started collections of their own because of him. Something you’d walked past a hundred times could stop you on the hundred-and-first.

Josh was a great father and the kind of friend who showed up when you needed him. I miss the happiness he brought with him. I miss talking with him, too. We could be deep in a conversation about something neither of us had an easy answer for, and then, on a whim, he’d say, “Hey, fuck it, let’s build our own lightsabers.” We both loved figuring out what a piece of scrap could become. Mine is still unfinished. I wish I could put it in his hands and ask him what he thinks.

Josh was one of the people who encouraged me along the way as I built Knightfall. His friendship helped me find some of that joy in making things again. The sea turtle in the Sanctuary carries his nickname, Crush, because I wanted a place here to remember my friend.

I think of all the people who still have a rock they picked up because of Josh. I hope that when they hold it, they can picture his smile. I know what I’ll think the next time a stone catches my eye. Josh would love this. I miss being able to show him.

— Ronnie
