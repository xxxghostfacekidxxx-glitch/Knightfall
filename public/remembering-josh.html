A letter for Josh

Josh and I could look at a pile of scrap metal and see lightsabers. We both loved taking things apart, picking out the pieces that caught our eye, and figuring out what else they could become. I never finished mine. I wish I could’ve put it in his hands, just once, and asked him what he thought.

Josh got so many people collecting rocks and crystals because being excited about something came naturally to him. After you knew Josh, you looked at the ground a little differently. Something you’d walked past a hundred times could stop you on the hundred-and-first.

When I was going through depression, Josh helped me find my way back to the kid who wanted to build a lightsaber out of spare parts just because it would be cool. I’d missed that kid. Getting that part of myself back helped me finish building Ash-Fall, and I want the people who come here to know whose friendship helped me do it.

Josh was a great father and a fucking great friend. If you needed him, he showed up. I miss you, Josh. I wish you could see this place.

The sea turtle in the Sanctuary carries your nickname, Crush. And if I ever finish that lightsaber, you’ll still be the first person I’ll wish I could show it to.

— Ronnie
