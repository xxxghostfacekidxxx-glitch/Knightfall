03 / Stencil Surgery: SVG tracing

Turn the loaded image into real vector paths using the locally hosted ImageTracerJS library. Use Blackwork for tattoo stencil starting points or Color for posterized artwork. Inspect paths and clean up the exported SVG in any vector editor before tattooing or printing.

Load an image to unlock vector tracing. Source pixels never leave this device.

Tracing is limited to 192 pixels on the longest input side to protect mobile devices. Vector results can be enlarged without bitmap blur but may require manual cleanup.

Measured stencil transfer sheet

Export a local print-ready SVG with your processed, mirrored artwork at the chosen physical size. Includes a 50 mm calibration line to verify the printed result with a ruler.

Process an image to enable a measured transfer sheet. Print with scaling set to 100%, not Fit to Page.

04 / Portal Sigils: QR share cards

Make a printable card pointing at your Knightfall profile, gallery, shop or portfolio. QR patterns are generated entirely offline by a locally hosted MIT-licensed library.

QR code scanning works offline after generating the card. Verify the target URL and test with a phone camera before printing.

The QR SVG has a plain black-on-white code for reliable scanning; the PNG includes the Knightfall art card. Never encode passwords, account tokens or private URLs.

05 / Commons Scout: Openverse

Find openly licensed images to inspire original tattoos, avatars and games. Openverse is a discovery index, not permission to copy every work. Always inspect the original license and required attribution.

Searching sends only the phrase and selected license filter from your device to Openverse. Your loaded artwork is never sent. Results link out to their original creators; no external image previews load automatically.
