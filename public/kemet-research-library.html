Cultural context

Ancient heritage and modern Egypt

Ancient Egyptian history belongs to the wider heritage of Egypt and connected regions. Modern Egyptians and their diverse institutions are active participants in its study and preservation.

Later history

Alexandria and Hellenistic learning

The city became a major intellectual and cultural center within a multilingual kingdom; it represents later developments, not a direct snapshot of the Old Kingdom.

Research method

Evaluating extraordinary claims

Claims of lost supertechnology, aliens or secret unbroken priesthoods require evidence. Known Egyptian achievements stand on their own and should not be diminished by unsupported explanations.
