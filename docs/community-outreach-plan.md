# Knightfall community outreach: zero-budget launch plan

Goal: recruit returning creators and players, not inflate registrations. Start with existing community channels and original content; no paid advertising or unsolicited bulk messages.

## Four-week schedule
- Week 1: publish a welcome thread, a creator introduction and a short original Chaos Cartridge gameplay clip. Invite existing members to report onboarding friction.
- Week 2: run a free sprite challenge using original or licensed art; showcase opt-in submissions with attribution.
- Week 3: host a free asynchronous card-game or arcade challenge; use non-monetary recognition, no purchase or chance-based prizes.
- Week 4: publish a development recap, highlight community contributions with consent, and prioritize the three most reported usability problems.

## Distribution
Use the Madhouse announcements, public site links and relevant external communities only where self-promotion is permitted. Ask moderators before posting promotional links. Do not scrape contacts, send unsolicited DMs, or publish private member information. Keep adult sections out of general-audience promotions.

## Measurement
Record weekly aggregate counts of new registrations, returning active members, first game starts, creator submissions and resolved onboarding reports where available. Do not invent unavailable metrics. Avoid tracking pixels and individual profiling; use existing aggregate platform data. Compare weekly results and retain only useful aggregate reporting.

## Success and stop rules
Establish a baseline in week one; aim for improved returning participation and fewer onboarding failures by week four. Stop any channel generating spam complaints. Do not spend money until the owner approves a separate budget and the community has demonstrated retention. This plan authorizes no posting or purchases.
