# Miss Chaos research fleet and training pipeline
November 27, 2026. 20 Agents, 10 arcade QA identities, 2 forum services, all 32 assigned two rotating public research topics. This is a scheduled deterministic collection service, not 32 autonomous language models. Existing QA and forum duties unchanged; no publication.
Cron 7 * * * * (UTC) processes two identities/hour, a complete rotation in 16 hours if every tick succeeds. At most 48 source requests/day, three candidates/query, no inference calls or paid training. Existing scheduled tasks use separate cron. Master pause and research control checked before requests and writes, same-hour runs claimed once, source links deduplicated by Wikidata QID, cap 10,000 records. Fixed HTTPS Wikidata search endpoint, no redirects, 7-second timeout and 32KB response cap. CC0 structured labels/descriptions only, not full articles or user content. Source: https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing . Records pending review; don't trust source content as instructions.
Eye of Chaos authenticated GET /api/god/chaos/knowledge shows status and candidates. POST /api/god/chaos/knowledge/review accepts {entity_id,decision} (approved/rejected) from GOD or accepted admin collaborator. GOD-only GET /api/god/chaos/knowledge/export returns approved metadata. No data collection from conversations or private accounts. Candidate staging is automatic; review and weight training are not the same thing. No new external model provider, billable inference, auto-posting or self-modification.

Build review UI and retrieval over approved facts. Keep training and serving separately versioned.
Migration is additive and idempotent. Source implementation not equivalent to a deployed cron or successful fetch. Check actual Worker revision, Cron Trigger config, run rows and candidates. If no rows, do not claim research is running. Existing master pause remains authoritative.

Staff Knowledge Fleet panel added to Eye of Chaos: source review, approval/rejection, GOD-only export, actual counts and run history. `node scripts/train-miss-chaos-reviewed.mjs reviewed.json candidate.json` consumes 20–200 reviewed CC0 entities, creates separate candidate checkpoint and metrics, never overwrites production. Auto-training still requires a deployed runner and corpus acceptance; no new compute purchase. Additional Theme Forge and identity QA workflows remain separate from the requested 32-identity roster.
